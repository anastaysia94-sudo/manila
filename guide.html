School letter in your hand. What should you organize first?

Manila is a preparation and documentation tool for families dealing with IEP, 504, evaluation, discipline, placement and other school paperwork. It helps create a usable record. It does not replace a lawyer, advocate, Parent Training and Information Center, or the current rules in your state.

What is an IEP meeting notice supposed to tell a parent?

Federal IDEA rules require public agencies to take steps to ensure parent participation, including notice early enough to give an opportunity to attend. The notice must state the purpose, time and location and identify who will attend. Source: 34 C.F.R. § 300.322.

How long can an initial IDEA evaluation take?

The federal baseline is completion within 60 days after parental consent, unless the state establishes its own timeframe. Exceptions exist. Source: 34 C.F.R. § 300.301.

When is a manifestation determination required?

For a disciplinary change of placement, the manifestation review occurs within 10 school days of the decision to change placement because of a code-of-conduct violation. It is not accurate to say every tenth removal day automatically creates the same legal event. Source: 34 C.F.R. § 300.530.

What is an IEE at public expense?

If a parent disagrees with an evaluation obtained by the public agency, IDEA provides a right to request an independent educational evaluation at public expense subject to the regulation. The agency must, without unnecessary delay, either seek due process to defend its evaluation or ensure the IEE is provided, subject to the rule's conditions. Source: 34 C.F.R. § 300.502.

What should I save from a school meeting?

Keep the notice, evaluations, current plan, your written concerns, service logs, notes about decisions, any Prior Written Notice, and the version of any draft or plan you were given. Date your own notes and separate what somebody actually said from your interpretation of it.

Where can I get real local help?

The federally supported Parent Center network can help families find a state Parent Training and Information Center or Community Parent Resource Center: Find your Parent Center.

What Manila does not know automatically

It does not automatically know your state's current deadline, local recording law, district procedure, whether a removal legally counts in a particular pattern, or the facts needed for legal advice. Treat generated wording as preparation material and verify jurisdiction-specific rules.
